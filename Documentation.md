# Warehouse Management Portal

## Project Overview

The Warehouse Management Portal is a web-based application developed to manage warehouse operations efficiently. It helps users maintain product details, monitor stock levels, manage suppliers, and handle customer orders in one system.

The main purpose of this project is to reduce manual work in warehouse management and make inventory tracking easier, faster, and more accurate.

## Problem Statement

In traditional warehouses, product and stock information is often maintained manually in registers or spreadsheets. This can lead to errors such as:

- Incorrect stock quantity
- Missing product information
- Difficulty in tracking suppliers
- Delay in processing orders
- Poor inventory management

This Warehouse Management Portal provides a digital solution to organize and manage warehouse data.

## Objectives

The objectives of this project are:

- To manage product information
- To maintain stock quantity details
- To manage supplier information
- To create and track customer orders
- To reduce manual warehouse management work
- To improve accuracy in inventory handling

## Features

The Warehouse Management Portal includes the following features:

### 1. Product Management

Users can add, update, delete, and view product details.

Product details may include:

- Product ID
- Product name
- Category
- Price
- Quantity
- Description

### 2. Inventory or Stock Management

This module helps to track available stock in the warehouse.

Users can:

- View current stock quantity
- Add new stock
- Update stock details
- Identify low-stock products
- Maintain inventory records

### 3. Supplier Management

Suppliers provide products to the warehouse. This feature helps manage supplier details.

Supplier details may include:

- Supplier ID
- Supplier name
- Contact number
- Email address
- Company name
- Address

### 4. Order Management

This module helps users manage customer orders.

Users can:

- Create new orders
- View order details
- Update order status
- Track order quantity
- Manage completed and pending orders

### 5. Dashboard

The dashboard provides a quick summary of warehouse information.

It can display:

- Total number of products
- Total stock available
- Number of suppliers
- Number of orders
- Low-stock alerts
